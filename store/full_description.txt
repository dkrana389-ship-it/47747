Keep Focus helps you stop endless doomscrolling on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

How It Works:
1. Create your personal profile with your life goal or dream and an optional photo.
2. Keep Focus discreetly counts continuous short videos you watch.
3. After 30 continuous reels (customizable in Settings), a full-screen motivational reminder appears with your dream and photo to help you close the app and get back to your goals.

Features:
- Tracks Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts locally.
- Clean Material 3 dashboard with daily stats and weekly bar chart.
- 100% private: Zero internet permission required. All profile data, photos, and stats stay strictly on your device.
